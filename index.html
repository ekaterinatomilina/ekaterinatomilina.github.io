About me

I obtained my PhD in applied mathematics in 2025 at Université Paris-Saclay. I hold a Master's degree in Statistical and Stochastic modelling from Université de Bordeaux, and a Bachelor's degree in Fundamental mathematics. I have taught several courses at Faculté des Sciences d'Orsay and at ENSTA as a Teaching Assistant during my PhD: Linear Algebra, Probability, Statistical modelling, Statistics for biology, and Fundamentals of Statistics (in English). My research mostly focuses on high-dimensional heterogeneous network inference via copula-based models, in a semi-parametric framework.
