About me

I recently obtained my PhD in Mathematics at the interfaces from Université Paris-Saclay.

My research focused on the inference of multi-omics regulatory networks using Gaussian copula models, as well as heterogeneous network modeling in a semi-parametric framework with latent variable structures.

I primarily work with Python and R, developing both methodological and applied statistical tools.

I previously completed a Master’s degree in Statistical and Stochastic Modeling at Université de Bordeaux in 2022, where I also worked in industry as an apprentice at Lumivero.
